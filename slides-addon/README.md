# Tab Order Manager - Slides Add-on

Tab Order Manager is a lightweight accessibility tool designed to solve a common hurdle in Google Slides: **managing the "tab order" of slide elements.**


## Why is this important?

In Google Slides, screen readers and assistive technologies navigate slide elements based on their **"z-index"** (the order in which they were added or layered). If you move objects around or add text boxes out of sequence, your presentation can become a confusing experience for users who rely on screen readers.

## Key Features

* **Intuitive Reordering:** Easily adjust the sequence of text boxes, images, and shapes without the headache of manual "Bring to Front" or "Send to Back" commands.
* **Sidebar Integration:** View the current tab order of all elements on your active slide in a clean, organized list.
* **Accessibility First:** Designed specifically to help students and faculty create presentations that are inclusive and compliant with accessibility standards.


## Privacy & Security

This is a **private, internal tool**. 

* All processing occurs locally within the Google Apps Script environment. 
* Tab Order Manager does **not** collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data or presentation content to external servers.
