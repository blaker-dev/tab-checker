# Privacy Policy: Tab Order Manager
### Effective Date: April 8, 2026

1. **Introduction:** This Privacy Policy describes how Tab Order Manager ("the App") handles information when you use the Google Slides add-on. My goal is to provide an accessibility tool while maintaining the highest standards of user privacy.

2. **Information Collection and Use:**
No Personal Data Collection: The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII).

    - **Presentation Access:** The App requires permission to "See, edit, create, and delete all your Google Slides presentations." This permission is used exclusively to read the coordinates of elements on your slides and adjust their z-index (tab order).

    - **Local Processing:** All script executions occur within the Google Apps Script environment. No data from your presentations is ever sent to external servers or third-party services.

3. **Data Storage and Security:**
Because the App does not collect data, there is no database where your information is stored. The App relies entirely on Google’s infrastructure for its operation and security.

4. **Information Sharing:**
I do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your information to outside parties. Your slide content remains entirely within your Google account.

5. **Third-Party Services:**
The App is built on the Google Workspace platform. Your use of the App is also governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

6. **User Control and Data Deletion:**
You can revoke the App's access to your Google account at any time by:

        Opening Google Slides.

        Navigating to Extensions > Add-ons > Manage add-ons.

        Selecting Tab Order Manager and choosing Uninstall.

7. **Changes to This Policy**
I may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in the App’s functionality or legal requirements. Any updates will be posted to this page with a revised "Effective Date."
